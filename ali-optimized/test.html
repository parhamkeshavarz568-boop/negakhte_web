    What to look for

    Normal mode:

    • Menu cards have frosted-glass blur
    • Background stars twinkle
    • Clouds drift across the sky
    • Legend paintings have a sepia tone

    Lite mode:

    • Menu cards have solid cream background (no blur)
    • Stars are still visible but not animated
    • Clouds are frozen in place
    • Paintings show in normal color, just faded
